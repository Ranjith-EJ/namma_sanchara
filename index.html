Ask us anything ↗
How can I book a trip? ＋

Choose an adventure and contact the team through WhatsApp or phone to confirm the date, availability and booking process.

Can solo travellers join? ＋

Yes. Group adventures can be a great way to meet new people. Please confirm the group format and availability with the organisers.

What should I carry for a trek? ＋

Comfortable shoes, water, weather-appropriate clothing, personal medication and any trip-specific items shared by the organisers.

Are trip prices and inclusions fixed? ＋

Prices, inclusions, transport, meals and entry fees can vary by trip. Ask for the latest written itinerary before paying.

What happens if the weather changes? ＋

Outdoor plans may change for safety. Confirm the organiser's cancellation, rescheduling and refund policy before booking.
